# About This Portfolio

I am a Data Scientist with a background in Applied Mathematics and Statistics, focused on applying machine learning, statistical modeling, optimization, and artificial intelligence to complex analytical problems. I hold an M.S. in Data Science & Analytics and a B.S. in Applied Mathematics with a minor in Applied Statistics from Grand Valley State University, and I am currently pursuing an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Johns Hopkins University.

This portfolio primarily highlights academic projects and research completed throughout my undergraduate and graduate studies, spanning several technical areas:

- **Machine Learning & Artificial Intelligence:** Classification, neural networks, computer vision, generative modeling, and model evaluation.
- **Statistical Modeling & Optimization:** Regression, hypothesis testing, predictive modeling, and mathematical optimization.
- **Geospatial Analysis:** Spatial data analysis, historical redlining, and geographic visualization.
- **Predictive Analytics & Time Series:** Forecasting, temporal patterns, and data-driven prediction.
- **Interactive Development & Visualization:** Data-driven applications, interactive visualizations, and analytical tools.
- **Research & Technical Writing:** Applied research exploring statistical methods, machine learning architectures, and AI-enabled systems.

Across these projects, I emphasize rigorous analytical methodology, interpretable results, and the practical application of statistical and computational techniques.
